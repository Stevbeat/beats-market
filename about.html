Meet StevBeatz

StevBeatz is a Ghana-based music producer, instrumentalist, and recording engineer specializing in Afrobeats, Highlife, Trap, and Hip-hop. Skilled in keyboard and guitar, he creates original beats, instrumentals, and loops that blend creativity with diverse musical influences.

His releases include Made In Me (2019), Friday Night (Amapiano Version) (2022), and FLORA (2023). In 2025, he collaborated with artists and producers including AMG Vimboy, King Cudjoe, Skeelo, Kofi Lil, Canti, Lexis Beatz, and Percuz, continuing to expand his sound and presence in the music industry.
