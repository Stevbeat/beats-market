Good to know

Quick answers.

A few helpful details before you start your next project.

What music services does StevBeatz offer?

Services include custom beat production, beat leasing and exclusive beat sales, audio mixing and mastering, vocal tuning and editing, full song production, sample packs and drum kits, jingles and background music, music production training, studio recording, music for films, adverts and online content, and instrument overdubbing or tracking.

Can I request a custom beat or full song production?

Message StevBeatz on WhatsApp to share your idea and creative direction. You can also use the contact form.

How do I ask about leasing or buying a beat exclusively?

Browse the beat previews and store listings. For questions or exclusive purchase terms, message StevBeatz on WhatsApp.

How can I book recording, mixing, or instrument tracking?

Choose a category above and message StevBeatz on WhatsApp to discuss recording, mixing and mastering, vocal editing, or instrument tracking. The contact form is also available.
