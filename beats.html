A little before the first take

Find your sound. Know your rights.

Practical notes for choosing an Afrobeats instrumental, licensing a beat, and working with a producer.

Choosing an Afrobeats instrumental

Start with the feeling and vocal idea you want to build around. Preview beats on this page without an account, then shortlist the ones that leave room for your melody, lyrics, and performance. If you have a reference track or a preferred tempo, mention it when you get in touch.

Listen to the beat previews ↗

What to check in a beat license

License terms differ by beat and seller. Before release, check the permitted uses, platforms, distribution or stream limits, credit requirements, Content ID rules, and whether the license is exclusive. Confirm what files are included and keep a copy of the agreed terms. Ask before purchasing if a term is unclear.

Ask about licensing terms ↗

How to work well with a music producer

Share a clear brief: your goal for the song, references, lyrics or a rough demo, any known key or tempo, your deadline, and the sound you want to avoid. Agree on deliverables, revisions, credits, and rights before work begins. Clear feedback helps the production move forward.
